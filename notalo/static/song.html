Frequently asked questions

Is this sheet music free to print and use?

Yes. {{TITLE}} is in the public domain and this arrangement was written and rendered by Notalo. Print it, share it, use it in lessons.

What do the colors mean?

Red letters are the right hand (treble clef), blue letters are the left hand (bass clef), green is the chord for the bar. This is the same output Notalo gives for any sheet music you upload.

Can I get it with Do Re Mi or numbers instead of letters?

Yes — download the image above, upload it to Notalo, and pick solfège (Do Re Mi), German letters, or numbers (1 2 3) as the label style.
