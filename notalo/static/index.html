Notalo vs. MusicXML-Based Tools — Two Ways to Add Note Names

Most note-naming tools require a MusicXML file to start. Notalo works directly from a photo or PDF. Here's how the two approaches compare.

NotaloMusicXML-based tools
Input formatPDF, JPG, PNGMusicXML
Prep neededNone — upload the file as-isExport to MusicXML first (e.g. via MuseScore)
LabelingSolfège + letter names, 5 languagesVaries by tool (usually letter names)
Free usage1 use without signup + 2 more after signupVaries (often limited by saved-score count)
Save/shareNot applicable — files deleted after processingSome tools offer score libraries and sharing
Best forSheet music you only have as a photo, scan, or printMusicians already working in MusicXML

If your sheet music exists only as a photo, a PDF, or a printed page, Notalo skips the file-conversion step entirely. If you already work in MusicXML and want a score library with sharing, a MusicXML-based tool may fit better.

FAQ

What file formats are supported?

PDF, JPG, and PNG — sheet music files up to 20MB.

Do I need MusicXML or notation software?

No. Just upload a photo or PDF of your sheet music as-is — Notalo detects noteheads directly from the image.

Is it free?

Yes — one free try without signing up, plus 2 more after verifying your email. After that, credits are available at low one-time prices, no subscription.

What note-name languages are supported?

Korean (도레미), English (C D E), Italian (Do Re Mi), Japanese (ドレミ), and German (C D E H).

Are my files stored?

No. Uploaded files and results are deleted immediately after processing.
