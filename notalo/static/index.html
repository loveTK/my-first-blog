Notalo vs. MusicXML-Based Tools — Two Ways to Get Sheet Music with Letters

Most letter-notes tools require a MusicXML file to start. Notalo works directly from a photo or PDF. Here's how the two approaches compare.

NotaloMusicXML-based tools
Input formatPDF, JPG, PNGMusicXML

If your sheet music exists only as a photo, a PDF, or a printed page, Notalo skips the file-conversion step entirely. If you already work in MusicXML and want a score library with sharing, a MusicXML-based tool may fit better.

FAQ

What file formats are supported?

PDF, JPG, and PNG — sheet music files up to 20MB.

Do I need MusicXML or notation software?

No. Just upload a photo or PDF of your sheet music as-is — Notalo detects noteheads directly from the image.

Is it free?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up, credits, or payment required. The site is supported by ads.

Which letter and solfège systems are supported?

Letters (C D E), German letters (C D E H), Italian solfège (Do Re Mi), Japanese solfège (ドレミ), and numbers (1 2 3, C=1).

Which scores work best?

Clean, printed sheet music: a PDF exported from notation software or a sharp, straight scan. The model was trained on printed notation, so smudged copies, faded prints, hand-drawn scans and photos taken at an angle can miss or mislabel notes. If you have the original PDF, upload that.

Can I make easy piano sheet music for a beginner?

Yes. Upload the piece you already have — a photo, scan or PDF — and Notalo writes the letter (or solfège) under every note, so a beginner can play it without reading the staff first. Right hand and left hand are colored differently.

Can I convert sheet music to MIDI?

Yes. After conversion, download a practice MIDI of the detected notes with their lengths (whole to sixteenth, dotted) at any tempo. Rests and ties aren't detected, so use it to check pitches and sing along rather than as a finished arrangement.

Does it add chord symbols?

Yes. Notalo reads the notes in each measure, works out the chord (C, Am, G7…) and prints it in green above the staff — handy for accompaniment. It's a best guess from the written notes; turn it off with the checkbox if you don't need it.

Is Notalo a sheet music scanner?

Yes. It scans a photo, scan or PDF of sheet music, reads the notes, and gives you back the same sheet music with letters — plus chord symbols and a MIDI file of what it read. No app to install, free.

Are my files stored?

No. Uploaded files and results are deleted immediately after processing.
