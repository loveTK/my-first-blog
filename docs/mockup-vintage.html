Questions

Before you begin

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no credits. Ads keep it running.

Are my files stored?

No. Uploads and results are deleted the moment processing ends.

Can I get a MIDI file?

Yes. A practice MIDI with note lengths, at any tempo. Rests and ties are not read.

Which scores work best?

Printed notation: a PDF from notation software or a straight, sharp scan.
